# MWS Managed Kafka SLA

The public Managed Kafka SLA page describes availability conditions for multi-node
clusters. It states that the SLA applies to clusters with at least three brokers and at
least three controllers, with replication factor of three or more and a minimum number of
in-sync replicas of two or more.

The public page lists separate availability levels for read and write operations.
